# Our reproduced results

Store measurements from our own runs here, with the exact run configuration, source revision, data split, random seed, and evaluation definition for each result. Do not place the authors' published measurements in this folder.

The training scripts write one file per run under `data/vision/` or `data/translation/`, with translation time histories under `data/translation_history/`. Targeted-ablation runs go under `ablations/<id>/data/vision/`, separate from the 229 baseline files; see [ABLATIONS.md](../ABLATIONS.md). The shared columns and Arrow types match the authors' converted Parquet tables in `../their-results/hf_dataset/data/`; additional columns identify our run and its actual update/epoch. Vision errors are fractions; our translation token errors are percentages and unsmoothed token NLL is in nats. The authors' translation metric definitions are not fully verified, so matching column names alone does not prove exact metric equivalence.

Each run's `code/runs/.../config.json`, `run_metadata.json`, and `metrics.jsonl` define how its exported measurements were obtained. See [code/README.md](../code/README.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for the field-level contract. The export can be rebuilt from `metrics.jsonl` with `code/export_results.py` after a crash.

For the UCloud vision sweep, the Parquet files, datasets, checkpoints, and run records are written under mounted persistent `/work`. W&B stores metrics only.

The completed 400-epoch CIFAR-10 ResNet sweep from UCloud job `12403634` is downloaded to [`data/vision/`](data/vision/): 30 Parquet files, one for each of ten widths and three label-noise rates. Each file has measurements at epochs 10, 20, …, 400. The two 20%-noise runs at widths 3 and 64 were completed in the earlier job `12403590` and reused by this sweep. Full checkpoints and run records remain in the UCloud `/work/atdl-b200-benchmark-2026-09-26` directory. The [paired comparison notebook](../our-plots/resnet_ours_vs_published.ipynb) plots these files beside the authors' released CIFAR-10 ResNet results over the matched width and 400-epoch window.

The completed CIFAR-10 CNN sweep from UCloud job `12403687` is also downloaded to [`data/vision/`](data/vision/): 48 Parquet files for 16 widths and 0%, 10%, or 20% label noise. Every file has 40 measurements at 1,250-step intervals through **50,000 optimizer steps**, with no missing train or test errors. The one-seed runs used augmentation and SGD with inverse-square-root learning-rate decay. Full checkpoints and run records remain under `/work/atdl-b200-benchmark-2026-09-26/runs/sweeps/cnn-cifar10-sgd-50000-2026-09-27-10-37-31/`. The same [comparison notebook](../our-plots/resnet_ours_vs_published.ipynb) shows the augmented Figure 5 subset and the CNN width-128 Figure 10 histories beside the authors' data near the same 50,000-step horizon. Authors' steps are estimated from an unverified 256-step measurement interval.

The local vision folder now contains **229 audited Parquet files**. The additional completed grids are 30 augmented CIFAR-100 ResNets (`12403713`), 33 non-augmented CIFAR-10 SGD CNNs (`12403788`), 33 non-augmented CIFAR-10 Adam CNNs (`12403936`), 11 clean CIFAR-100 SGD CNNs (`12403784`), and 44 augmented CIFAR-10 SGD CNN subset runs at 12,500 or 25,000 examples (`12403801`). Every file has 40 distinct measurements, its planned final epoch or step, and non-missing train/test error and loss. All are single-seed results. The `figure-6` name of job `12403784` is misleading: its submitted configuration and result metadata identify it as CIFAR-100 CNN SGD, so it contributes to reduced Figure 7. The separate Adam job supplies the reduced Figure 6 comparison. See [EXPERIMENTS.md](../EXPERIMENTS.md) for figure coverage and remaining gaps.

The [A100 timing pilot](pilots/vision-pilot-2026-09-26-18-41-30/REPORT.md) contains 36 two-epoch CIFAR runs and GPU-hour estimates for the larger grids. Its local Parquet endpoints are excluded from Git; the report and timing summary are kept. The pilot is separate from the completed reproduction results.

[Completed sweep timing records](completed-sweep-timings.json) give sanitized UCloud RUNNING/SUCCESS timestamps, B200 allocations, and elapsed times for the seven completed sweeps. The 14.98 GPU-hour total excludes pilots and prior experiments.
