# Deep Double Descent: complete run plan for Figures 1–12

One run is one full `(dataset, subset, label-noise mask, architecture, width, augmentation, optimizer, initialization seed)` training trajectory. Endpoint curves, epoch curves, and heatmaps can reuse that trajectory when train/test metrics are saved during training. The table below is a **concrete reproduction target**, based on the paper and locally audited released grids. A released width list establishes coordinates, not that every condition and seed combination was completed by the authors.

## Shared training protocol

- **Images:** CIFAR-10/100, batch size 128, cross-entropy. Preactivation ResNet18 and five-layer CNN use channel widths `[k,2k,4k,8k]`. Augmentation is padded random crop plus horizontal flip. Label noise replaces a fixed fraction of training labels once with uniformly sampled *incorrect* classes. Default Adam is constant LR `1e-4` for **4,000 epochs**. Default SGD has no momentum, initial LR `0.1`, inverse-square-root decay updated every 512 steps, and **500,000 optimizer steps**. Clean CIFAR-100 CNN uses **1,000,000 SGD steps**. No weight decay or dropout. Preserve actual global step and epoch, train/test error and loss, subset IDs, corruption masks, and seeds.
- **Subsets:** Smaller datasets retain the **same 500K optimizer-step horizon**, so they undergo more dataset-equivalent passes. At batch size 128, 12,500 and 50,000 samples correspond to roughly 5,120 and 1,280 passes, respectively. Appendix B.4 calls Figure 11(a)'s horizon “500K epochs,” conflicting with the main step-based SGD protocol. This plan uses 500K **steps** provisionally; original run configs must settle that discrepancy.
- **Translation:** Six-layer encoder-decoder Transformer, eight heads, feed-forward dimension `4d`, BPE, 10% label smoothing, no dropout, Adam with warmup/inverse-square-root schedule, **80,000 optimizer steps**. Save train and held-out per-token loss/perplexity throughout. Appendix B.1 says “80 gradient steps,” conflicting with Section 4/Figure 8's 80K; 80K is the planning choice. Released CSVs contain endpoints only and have unresolved loss units/optimizer provenance.

**Job-name correction:** UCloud job `12403784` is named `figure-6`, but its submitted batch script and saved result metadata show **CIFAR-100 CNN with SGD**. It contributes to reduced Figure 7. The CIFAR-10 Adam arm for Figure 6 came from the separate, audited job `12403936`.

These are reduced horizons and width grids. The paper uses 4,000 epochs for Adam ResNets and CIFAR-10 CNNs, 500,000 steps for CIFAR-10 SGD CNNs, and 1,000,000 steps for the clean CIFAR-100 CNN. The [comparison notebook](our-plots/resnet_ours_vs_published.ipynb) distinguishes our recorded epochs/steps from the authors' measurement indices. For CNN comparisons near 50,000 SGD steps, 256 steps per author measurement is an **unverified estimate** from the released 1,952-point series and the stated 500,000-step protocol. Do not interpret these as exactly aligned trajectories.

At width 16, our augmented CIFAR-10 CNN's 20%-noise test error is **40.97%, 31.19%, and 21.74%** for 12,500, 25,000, and 50,000 training examples at step 50,000. The corresponding train errors are **10.71%, 25.89%, and 29.67%** against corrupted labels. These are single-seed observations. The 50,000-example augmented sweep also shows a 20%-noise test-error rise from **21.74% at width 16** to **28.11% at width 48**, then **25.88% at width 128**; longer training and repeats are needed to assess the paper's full pattern.
